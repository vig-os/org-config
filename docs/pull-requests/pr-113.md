# [PR 113](https://github.com/vig-os/org-config/pull/113) chore(config): retire orphaned APP_SYNC_ISSUES org secrets

Removes the `vig-os` **organization** secrets `APP_SYNC_ISSUES_ID` and
`APP_SYNC_ISSUES_PRIVATE_KEY` from `otterdog/vig-os/vig-os.jsonnet`.

## Why

A 2026-08-07 sweep of every `sync-issues.yml` in the org found the org-level
pair has **zero consumers**:

- The only workflows naming them are `vig-os/tessera`'s, and `tessera` carries
  its **own repo-level secrets of the same names** — a different App
  (`tessera-sync-issues-bot`, `app_id` 4483218). A repo-level secret always
  shadows an org-level one, so nothing ever resolved to the org pair.
- Every other repo's `sync-issues.yml` authenticates with `COMMIT_APP_*`.

`tessera`'s `orgs.newRepoSecret('APP_SYNC_ISSUES_*')` declarations (~line 665)
are deliberately **untouched**; the comment beside them already warns against
"deduplicating" them into org secrets.

## Expected plan

`0 to add, 1 to change, 2 to delete` — the standing `vs-dolt` change, plus the
two now-undeclared live org secrets. `otterdog apply` does not delete secrets,
so those two are removed live out-of-band after merge and then drop out of the
plan.

Phase 1 of the App-secret consolidation program (#112).

Refs: #111
